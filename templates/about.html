ABOUT US

Connecting blood donors with people who need them.

Smart Blood Donation Network is a platform designed to simplify the process of finding compatible and eligible blood donors when blood is needed.

OUR PURPOSE

Making blood donation easier and more organized

The platform connects recipients and donors through blood-group compatibility, donor eligibility checks and location-based prioritization.

🩸

Blood Compatibility

Blood requests are matched with compatible donor blood groups.

✓

Donor Eligibility

The system checks donor eligibility before recommending them for a request.

📍

Location Priority

Eligible donors from the same city are prioritized before expanding the search.

HOW IT WORKS

A simple process

The network connects recipients and donors through a few straightforward steps.

1

Create a Request

A recipient creates a blood request with the required blood group, units, hospital and city.

→
2

Find Compatible Donors

Donor Responds
