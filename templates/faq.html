FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Smart Blood Donation Network.

Who can register as a donor?

Users can register as donors and provide their blood group, date of birth, weight, gender and city information.

How are compatible donors found?

The system checks the blood group required by the recipient and identifies compatible donor blood groups.

How is donor eligibility checked?

The system checks factors such as donor availability, age, weight and the waiting period after the previous donation.

Why are same-city donors shown first?

The system prioritizes eligible donors from the same city before expanding the search to other cities.

What happens when a donor accepts?

The donor's response is recorded with the blood request. The recipient can then view the accepted donor and record the completed donation.

Is donation history recorded?

Yes. Completed donations are recorded in the donor's donation history.

Why can't a donor donate again immediately?

After a donation, the donor remains in the system's waiting period before becoming eligible for another donation.

Can a donor reject a request?

Yes. A donor can reject a blood request. Other eligible compatible donors can still respond to the request.
